# German Select - Market Research & Competitive Pricing Analysis
## Medical Tourism: Hurghada (Egypt) vs Turkey - January 2026

---

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive market analysis examines competitive pricing across **WhatClinic**, **PlacidWay**, **Bookimed**, **Flymedi**, and other leading medical tourism platforms for Hurghada, Egypt and Turkey. The findings inform strategic pricing adjustments for German Select's SelectCareOS platform.

### Key Findings
- **Turkey dominates** the medical tourism market with aggressive pricing (€2,600-€6,000 for bariatric surgery)
- **Egypt offers competitive pricing** ($1,500-$4,800 range) but lacks luxury all-inclusive packages
- **German Select's unique value proposition** (German MDs + Red Sea recovery + IoT/digital) justifies premium pricing
- **Recommended strategy**: Position as **premium differentiated** offering, not price competitor
